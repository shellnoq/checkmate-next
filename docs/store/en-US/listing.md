## Full description (4000 characters)

CheckMate Next brings a genuine chess experience with the Stockfish engine running
directly on your device. Choose from eight levels — from an opponent that hangs
pieces for absolute beginners, to the engine at full strength that human players
cannot realistically beat.

COMPLETE CHESS RULES
Castling, en passant, pawn promotion, the fifty-move rule, threefold repetition
and insufficient-material draws are all implemented. Checkmate and stalemate are
distinguished correctly.

EIGHT DIFFICULTY LEVELS
• Beginner (~600) — for players learning the pieces
• Novice (~900) — spots simple tactics
• Amateur (~1200) — calculates short combinations
• Club Player (~1500) — will not hand you material
• Candidate Master (~1800) — strong positional judgement
• Expert (~2100) — deep calculation
• National Master (~2400) — converts small edges
• Grandmaster — engine at full strength

TIME CONTROLS
Unlimited play plus bullet, blitz, rapid and classical presets. Fischer
increment is supported, and the clock stays accurate across backgrounding.

ANALYSIS TOOLS
The evaluation bar shows who stands better at a glance. The hint button draws
the engine's suggested move as an arrow on the board. Step back through the move
list to review any position, and export any game as PGN.

TWO PLAYERS
Play across one device. Everything from castling to promotion works exactly as
in a real game.

PERSONALISATION
Five board themes, three piece sets, light and dark modes. Coordinates, legal
move markers and the evaluation bar can each be toggled.

TURKISH AND ENGLISH
Switch instantly from inside the app.

NO INTERNET REQUIRED
The engine runs entirely on your device. The app collects no data, sends no
data, and contains no advertising.
